RahisiBook Events: platform fee

Listing a free event costs nothing. For a paid event, the platform fee is 2% of the ticket price multiplied by the number of tickets you plan to sell, unless we agree a different rate with you in writing or on your account. You pay the fee to RahisiBook's till, using the event name as the reference. A paid event goes live on the public page once we have recorded the fee as received, unless we have agreed otherwise with you. If you raise the price or the planned number of tickets, the fee is recalculated and the event may be hidden until the difference is paid. Platform fees are not refundable once an event has gone live, unless we agree otherwise.

RahisiBook Events: ticket buyers

When you reserve a ticket you are buying from the organizer, not from RahisiBook. Follow the organizer's payment instructions and use your ticket code as the reference. Your ticket is confirmed when the organizer records your payment. Refunds, changes and cancellations are handled by the organizer, and you should contact them first. Keep your ticket code private, because anyone who has it can open the ticket.
