How I got here

I started by building websites that make giving easy for nonprofits, under the name Donor Ready. A clear donation page can be the difference between a supporter giving and giving up, and I like making that moment simple.

Spas, salons and parlours have the same problem. A client who wants to book should not have to chase replies in DMs. That is why I built RahisiBook: booking, but rahisi (easy).

What I do

Booking systems for spas, salons and parloursA booking page for your clients, a "My bookings" page to check status, an owner dashboard, and WhatsApp confirmations. One-time setup fee of KES 15,000.
Websites for nonprofitsDonation-focused sites, like the one I built for Touching Lives Outreach, a Los Angeles County nonprofit that clothes and feeds families.

How I work

  • Phone first. Most of your clients will book from their phone, so I design for small screens first.
  • Simple and fast. Plain, quick pages that load well on mobile data.
  • Fair prices. Standard market prices, with no surprises.
  • One person, start to finish. No hand-offs between teams.
