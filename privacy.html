Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

RahisiBook builds and installs online booking systems for salons, spas and parlours in Kenya, and runs RahisiBook Events, a place to find events and buy tickets. This page explains what information we handle and why.

Information we collect

  • From you (a business owner): your name, business name, phone number, email address, and the services, prices and hours you ask us to set up.
  • From your clients, through your booking page: name, phone number, the service chosen, and the date and time booked.
  • From event organizers (RahisiBook Events): your name or company, phone number, email address, how customers pay you (such as a till, paybill or phone number), and the details, photos, prices and ticket numbers of the events you post.
  • From ticket buyers (RahisiBook Events): name, phone number, the event, the number of tickets, the ticket code, and when the booking was made and whether it was paid or used. You do not need an account to book.

How we use it

  • To set up, run and support your booking system.
  • To show bookings to you and to the client who made them.
  • To contact you about your project, for example on WhatsApp or by email.
  • To list events, reserve and show tickets, and let organizers check guests in at the door.
  • To review new organizers and to work out and record platform fees.

We do not sell personal information.

Your clients' data

The spa or salon decides what to do with its clients' booking details. RahisiBook handles that data only to provide the booking system. If you are a client and want your details corrected or removed, please contact the spa or salon first, or write to us and we will help.

Events and tickets

  • Who sees what. The event page shows the event details, the organizer's name and how to pay them. When you book, the organizer of that event can see your name, phone number, number of tickets and ticket code, so they can confirm your payment and let you in. We can see the same information to run the service.
  • Your ticket. You can see your tickets by entering the phone number you booked with. Anyone who has your ticket code or ticket link can open that ticket, so please do not share it publicly.
  • Payments. You pay the organizer directly, for example by M-Pesa. RahisiBook does not receive or hold the money for tickets, and we do not collect your M-Pesa PIN or card details.
  • The organizer is responsible for how they use your details. Organizers should use them only for your booking and the event. If you want your details corrected or removed, contact the organizer or write to us and we will help.

Sharing

We use third-party services, such as hosting, database and messaging providers (for example WhatsApp), to run the system. They process information only so the service can work. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Keeping information safe and how long

We use reasonable measures to protect information and keep it only as long as needed to provide the service or meet legal duties. Business owners can ask us to delete their data when they stop using the service.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, and you may object to how it is used. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Cookies and local storage

Our website stores small items in your browser, such as your light or dark theme and whether you dismissed the cookie notice. We do not use them to track you across other sites.

Changes
